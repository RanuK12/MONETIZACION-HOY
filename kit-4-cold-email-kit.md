## The REPLY Framework

Every good cold email has 5 parts. No more, no less.

**R — Relevance** (Line 1): Why you, why now. Prove you did your homework.
**E — Empathy** (Line 2): Name the pain they have. Make them nod.
**P — Proof** (Line 3): One specific result. No adjectives, just numbers.
**L — Light ask** (Line 4): Micro-commitment. Never "hop on a call."
**Y — You decide** (Line 5): Opt-out sentence. Counterintuitive but works.

Rule: Under 90 words. If it scrolls on mobile, it dies.

## Section 1 — Cold Outreach (Agency → SaaS)

### Template 1.1 — The "Noticed" Opener

```
Subject: Quick thought on {{Company}}'s pricing page

Hey {{FirstName}},

Noticed {{Company}} updated the pricing page last week — smart move dropping the "Enterprise" tier above the fold.

We did the same experiment for {{SimilarCompany}} last quarter. Their demo bookings went from 22/week to 61/week in 3 weeks.

Worth a 10-min Loom on what we found?

If it's not a priority right now, no worries — just reply "later" and I'll check back in Q3.

— {{YourName}}
```

**Why it works:** Shows you actually looked at their site. Specific number. Easy out.

### Template 1.2 — The "Mutual Pattern"

```
Subject: {{Competitor}} vs {{Company}}

{{FirstName}},

Been tracking the {{Industry}} space for 3 months. {{Competitor}} just launched a {{feature}} — you probably saw it.

We helped {{ClientA}} and {{ClientB}} ship the same thing in 5 weeks each.

Curious how you're thinking about it?

— {{YourName}}
```

### Template 1.3 — The "Wrong Person"

```
Subject: Wrong person?

{{FirstName}} —

Quick one: I'm looking for whoever owns outbound at {{Company}}. If that's not you, no worries — any chance you can point me to them?

If it is you: we built a system that took {{ClientCompany}} from 2% to 14% reply rates in 6 weeks. Happy to share the teardown.

— {{YourName}}
```

## Section 2 — Freelance Pitch (Any Industry)

### Template 2.1 — The Portfolio Drop

```
Subject: {{Project}} is 3 clicks from converting more

Hey {{FirstName}},

Clicked through {{Company}}'s onboarding yesterday. The value prop is strong but the CTA on step 3 reads "Continue" — that's leaving money on the table.

Rewrote it for a similar product ({{similar link}}) and conversion went from 6.1% → 11.3% in 12 days.

Can I send you a quick 4-minute Loom with 3 specific changes?

— {{YourName}}
```

### Template 2.2 — The Audit Hook

```
Subject: Free audit for {{Company}} (no catch)

{{FirstName}},

I do 2 free audits a week. Nothing fancy — I pick a page, record a 10-min Loom, hand it over.

No follow-up pitch unless you ask.

Want yours this week?

— {{YourName}}
```

### Template 2.3 — The Specific Defect

```
Subject: your {{page}} has a bug

{{FirstName}} — just a heads up: your {{specific page}} is loading the hero image at 2.4 MB on mobile. That's 4 seconds on 4G.

Easy fix (I can send the exact crop spec if helpful).

Not a pitch, just wanted to flag it.

— {{YourName}}
```

## Section 4 — Follow-Ups (This Is Where Money Is Made)

**Rule:** 80% of replies come after the first message. Follow up or die.

### Follow-up 1 (Day 3)

```
Subject: re: {{original subject}}

Bumping this in case it got buried.

Short version: {{one-line value prop}}.

Worth a 10-min chat?

— {{YourName}}
```

### Follow-up 2 (Day 7)

```
Subject: re: {{original subject}}

{{FirstName}} —

Last note from me on this. If it's not a fit, totally fine — just reply "pass" and I'll stop.

If it is: here's a 4-minute Loom breaking down what I'd do for {{Company}}: {{link}}.

— {{YourName}}
```

### Follow-up 3 (Day 14) — The Breakup

```
Subject: Closing the loop

{{FirstName}}, I'm going to close this thread — don't want to be the guy who keeps emailing.

If priorities shift and you want to revisit, here's my Calendly: {{link}}.

Good luck with {{currentInitiative}}.

— {{YourName}}
```

## Section 6 — The Ignore List (Never Use These)

18 phrases that torch your reply rate:

1. "I hope this email finds you well"
2. "Just circling back"
3. "Touching base"
4. "Pick your brain"
5. "Synergy"
6. "At your earliest convenience"
7. "Per my last email"
8. "Hope you had a great weekend"
9. "I wanted to reach out because"
10. "Is this the right person?"
11. "Briefly"
12. "I'll keep this short"
13. "Congratulations on the new role!" (unless you know them)
14. "Let me know if this makes sense"
15. "Does that work for you?"
16. "Looking forward to your reply"
17. "Thanks in advance"
18. "Sorry to bother you"

Rule of thumb: if you'd never say it out loud at a bar, don't write it.

## Section 7 — AI Personalization Prompts

Paste into Claude or ChatGPT with the prospect's LinkedIn profile or company page.

```

- Be under 15 words
- Reference something SPECIFIC from their profile (not generic)
- Feel human, not like a template
- Lead naturally into a pitch for {{YOUR OFFER}}

{{PASTE LINKEDIN HERE}}
```

### Prompt 2 — Pain Point Extractor

```
Read the company website below. List the 3 biggest operational pains 
this company is likely facing based on:
- Their team size (guess from LinkedIn)
- Their tech stack (infer from site)
- Their stage (infer from funding / traction signals)

For each pain, write a one-sentence cold email opener that names it without sounding presumptuous.

Company:
{{PASTE URL OR COPY}}
```

### Prompt 3 — Subject Line Tester

```
Write 10 cold email subject lines for this email body. 
Constraints:
- Max 6 words
- Lowercase except proper nouns
- Must not sound like marketing
- Should create curiosity, not promise value

Email body:
{{PASTE YOUR EMAIL}}
```

## Playbook — How to Use This Kit

**Week 1:** Pick 1 template. Send to 50 prospects. Track opens + replies.
**Week 2:** A/B test the subject line. Keep the winner.
**Week 3:** Add follow-up 1. Measure lift.
**Week 4:** Add follow-up 2. Measure again.
**Week 5+:** Roll out the winner at scale.

**Tools you need:**
- Any email tool (Gmail works)
- A spreadsheet for tracking
- 20 min a day

**Metrics that matter:**
- Open rate: target 40%+
- Reply rate: target 8%+
- Meeting booked rate: target 2%+

If your reply rate is under 3% after 200 sends, the problem is the list, not the email.

## Bonus — The Single Biggest Lesson

The best cold email doesn't sell. It **starts a conversation about a problem the prospect already knows they have.**

Your job is not to convince them. Your job is to show up with a sharp insight, be easy to respond to, and make it obvious you're not wasting their time.

Everything in this kit is in service of that.
