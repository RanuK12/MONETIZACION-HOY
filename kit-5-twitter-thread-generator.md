## Part 1 — Why Most Threads Die

Your thread dies in the first 3 seconds. Specifically, between the hook and tweet 2.

This is called "the drop." Twitter's algorithm watches:
- Scroll speed on tweet 1 (hook retention)
- Click rate to tweet 2 (unroll / see more)
- Save / reply / quote within 10 minutes (signal)

If the drop is over 60%, the thread is dead. No amount of good content saves it.

**Fix:** stop optimizing the whole thread. Optimize the hook and tweet 2 only. Everything else is detail.

## Part 3 — The Thread Engine (7-Part Structure)

**1. Hook** (tweet 1)
Use any of the 30 formats above. Must promise specific value. Must fit in 1 screen.

**2. The bridge** (tweet 2)
Credibility + context. Why should they listen to you? One-liner. No resume dumps.

**3. The problem** (tweet 3)
Name the pain they have. Use their internal language. Make them nod.

**4. The frame** (tweet 4)
The lens you're teaching. A framework, a principle, a rule.

**5. The meat** (tweets 5–N)
Execution. One idea per tweet. Use concrete examples. Numbers > adjectives.

**6. The recap** (second-to-last tweet)
3-line summary of the thread. Tweet-sized.

**7. The ask** (last tweet)
One clear CTA:
- Follow for more
- Save this thread
- DM me "{{keyword}}" for {{freebie}}
- Reply with your experience

Never two CTAs. Pick one.

## Part 4 — The "Save Trigger" Checklist

People save threads that:

☐ Have a framework with a name
☐ Include a template they'll use later
☐ Have a numbered list (5, 7, or 10 works best)
☐ Reference a specific tool or person
☐ Include a checklist
☐ Have a "Before/After" visual logic
☐ Cite a source (book, study, account)
☐ Promise a single outcome
☐ Use "copy-paste" or "steal this" language
☐ Include exact numbers (not ranges)
☐ End with a clear "next step"
☐ Have a downloadable / linked resource
☐ Answer a question the reader has at 2 AM
☐ Replace a paid course they've considered

Hit 6+ checkboxes → save rate spikes.
Hit 10+ → algo distribution.

## Part 7 — The Timing Playbook

**When to post:**
- Tuesday / Wednesday / Thursday
- 7-9 AM ET OR 12-1 PM ET
- Never Sunday (worst day)
- Never after 10 PM (engagement crater)

**First 30 minutes (critical window):**
- Reply to every comment
- Quote-tweet 1-2 of your best replies
- Do NOT edit the thread (algo penalty)
- If someone asks a good question, answer in a new tweet AND reply

**First 24 hours:**
- If it hits >5% engagement, reply with "if you enjoyed this, my newsletter goes deeper: {{link}}"
- If it's dying, don't panic — drop it, write the next one

**Week 2+:**
- Repost top threads with small tweaks every 2 weeks
- Turn best threads into blog posts / LinkedIn posts / newsletter issues
- Build a "greatest hits" pinned tweet every quarter

**Day 1:** Pick 1 hook format. Write 1 thread. Post it.

**Week 3:** Introduce a named framework. Repeat it in every thread.
