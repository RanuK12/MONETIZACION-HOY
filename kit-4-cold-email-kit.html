The REPLY Framework

Every good cold email has 5 parts. No more, no less.

R — Relevance (Line 1): Why you, why now. Prove you did your homework. E — Empathy (Line 2): Name the pain they have. Make them nod. P — Proof (Line 3): One specific result. No adjectives, just numbers. L — Light ask (Line 4): Micro-commitment. Never "hop on a call." Y — You decide (Line 5): Opt-out sentence. Counterintuitive but works.

Rule: Under 90 words. If it scrolls on mobile, it dies.

Section 1 — Cold Outreach (Agency → SaaS)

Template 1.1 — The "Noticed" Opener

Subject: Quick thought on {{Company}}'s pricing page

Hey {{FirstName}},

Noticed {{Company}} updated the pricing page last week — smart move dropping the "Enterprise" tier above the fold.

We did the same experiment for {{SimilarCompany}} last quarter. Their demo bookings went from 22/week to 61/week in 3 weeks.

Worth a 10-min Loom on what we found?

If it's not a priority right now, no worries — just reply "later" and I'll check back in Q3.

— {{YourName}}

Why it works: Shows you actually looked at their site. Specific number. Easy out.

Template 1.2 — The "Mutual Pattern"

Subject: {{Competitor}} vs {{Company}}

{{FirstName}},

Been tracking the {{Industry}} space for 3 months. {{Competitor}} just launched a {{feature}} — you probably saw it.

We helped {{ClientA}} and {{ClientB}} ship the same thing in 5 weeks each.

Curious how you're thinking about it?

— {{YourName}}

Template 1.3 — The "Wrong Person"

Subject: Wrong person?

{{FirstName}} —

Quick one: I'm looking for whoever owns outbound at {{Company}}. If that's not you, no worries — any chance you can point me to them?

If it is you: we built a system that took {{ClientCompany}} from 2% to 14% reply rates in 6 weeks. Happy to share the teardown.

— {{YourName}}

Section 4 — Follow-Ups (This Is Where Money Is Made)

Rule: 80% of replies come after the first message. Follow up or die.

Follow-up 1 (Day 3)

Short version: {{one-line value prop}}.

Worth a 10-min chat?

— {{YourName}}

Section 6 — The Ignore List (Never Use These)

18 phrases that torch your reply rate:

  1. "I hope this email finds you well"
  2. "Just circling back"
  3. "Touching base"
  4. "Pick your brain"
  5. "Synergy"
  6. "At your earliest convenience"
  7. "Per my last email"
  8. "Hope you had a great weekend"
  9. "I wanted to reach out because"
  10. "Is this the right person?"
  11. "Briefly"
  12. "I'll keep this short"
  13. "Congratulations on the new role!" (unless you know them)
  14. "Let me know if this makes sense"
  15. "Does that work for you?"
  16. "Looking forward to your reply"
  17. "Thanks in advance"
  18. "Sorry to bother you"

Rule of thumb: if you'd never say it out loud at a bar, don't write it.

Playbook — How to Use This Kit

Week 1: Pick 1 template. Send to 50 prospects. Track opens + replies. Week 2: A/B test the subject line. Keep the winner. Week 3: Add follow-up 1. Measure lift. Week 4: Add follow-up 2. Measure again. Week 5+: Roll out the winner at scale.

Tools you need: - Any email tool (Gmail works) - A spreadsheet for tracking - 20 min a day

Metrics that matter: - Open rate: target 40%+ - Reply rate: target 8%+ - Meeting booked rate: target 2%+

If your reply rate is under 3% after 200 sends, the problem is the list, not the email.

Bonus — The Single Biggest Lesson

The best cold email doesn't sell. It starts a conversation about a problem the prospect already knows they have.

Your job is not to convince them. Your job is to show up with a sharp insight, be easy to respond to, and make it obvious you're not wasting their time.

Everything in this kit is in service of that.
