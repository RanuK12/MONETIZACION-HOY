## PART 1: THE ANSWER FRAMEWORK — STAR-P METHOD

Every great interview answer follows this structure:

**S — Situation** (Set the scene, 1-2 sentences)
**T — Task** (What was your responsibility)
**A — Action** (What YOU specifically did — use "I", not "we")
**R — Result** (Quantified outcome: %, $, time saved, rank)
**P — Pivot** (Connect to THIS job: "This is why I want to apply that here...")

### Example:
**Question:** "Tell me about a time you managed a difficult project."

**S:** "At [Company], we were launching a new product with 3 weeks left and 2 team members out sick."
**T:** "As project lead, I had to deliver on time without the original team."
**A:** "I reprioritized the roadmap to cut non-essential features, brought in 1 contractor, and ran daily 15-min standups to catch blockers early."
**R:** "We launched on time. The MVP had 80% of planned features and hit 94% of our target KPIs in month 1."
**P:** "That experience taught me that constraints force creativity. It's actually how I approach product decisions — which is why this role at [Company] excites me."

## PART 2: 50 QUESTION TEMPLATES WITH ANSWER FRAMEWORKS

### BEHAVIORAL QUESTIONS (STAR-P Method)

1. **"Tell me about yourself."**
Framework: Present role → key achievement → past that led here → why this role.
```
"I'm currently [role] at [company] where I [key responsibility]. 
Most recently, I [specific achievement with number]. 
Before that, I [relevant background]. 
I'm here because [specific reason this role/company appeals to you]."
```

2. **"Why do you want to work here?"**
Framework: Specific company insight → how your skills match → your goal.
```
"Three things specifically drew me: [thing 1 — research-based], 
[thing 2 — mission/product alignment], and [thing 3 — role fit]. 
This aligns directly with where I want to grow in [area]."
```

3. **"Tell me about your greatest achievement."**
Framework: Choose something quantifiable. Use STAR-P.
```
"The achievement I'm most proud of is [result with number]. 
Here's how it happened: [STAR-P story in 90 seconds]."
```

4. **"Tell me about a failure."**
Framework: Real failure → what you learned → how you changed → result after.
```
"I once [specific failure]. It happened because [honest root cause]. 
What I learned: [lesson]. Since then, I've [specific behavior change] — 
and [positive outcome that resulted]."
```

5. **"Describe a time you handled conflict."**
Framework: Describe conflict neutrally → your approach → resolution → relationship outcome.
```
"I had a disagreement with [role, not name] about [topic]. 
Rather than escalating, I [specific action]. 
We resolved it by [method], and [positive outcome]. 
The relationship actually [improved/continued well] after."
```

6. **"How do you handle pressure/tight deadlines?"**
7. **"Give an example of leading without authority."**
8. **"Tell me about a time you influenced someone to change their mind."**
9. **"Describe your biggest weakness."**
10. **"Tell me about a time you went above and beyond."**
11. **"How do you prioritize when everything is urgent?"**
12. **"Describe a time you had to learn something quickly."**
13. **"Tell me about a time you made a decision with incomplete information."**
14. **"How have you handled a difficult team member?"**
15. **"Tell me about a time you received critical feedback."**

### COMPETENCY QUESTIONS

16. **"How do you approach problem-solving?"**
Framework: Process (steps) → example → result.

17. **"How do you manage multiple projects?"**
Framework: System/tool → prioritization method → example.

18. **"How do you stay current in your field?"**
Framework: Specific sources → recent thing you learned → how you applied it.

19. **"Describe your management style."**
Framework: Principle + example + outcome.

20. **"How do you build relationships with stakeholders?"**

### ROLE-SPECIFIC QUESTIONS

**For technical roles:**
21. "Walk me through your approach to debugging a complex issue."
22. "How do you ensure code quality under time pressure?"
23. "Describe a technical decision you made that you'd change now."
24. "How do you communicate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders?"
25. "Tell me about the most technically challenging project you've worked on."

**For leadership/management roles:**
26. "How do you develop people on your team?"
27. "Tell me about a time you had to let someone go."
28. "How do you build a high-performing team from scratch?"
29. "How do you handle an underperformer?"
30. "How do you give difficult feedback?"

**For sales/marketing roles:**
31. "Walk me through a deal/campaign you're most proud of."
32. "How do you handle rejection?"
33. "Tell me about a time you hit a target others thought was impossible."
34. "How do you build a pipeline from zero?"
35. "Describe your process for understanding a customer's needs."

### SITUATIONAL QUESTIONS ("What would you do if...")

36. "You're given a project with an impossible deadline — what do you do?"
37. "Your manager gives you feedback you disagree with — how do you respond?"
38. "A key team member leaves mid-project — how do you handle it?"
39. "You discover a major mistake in a project that's about to launch — what do you do?"
40. "Two executives give you conflicting priorities — how do you navigate this?"

### CLOSING QUESTIONS

41. "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"
Framework: Growth in this function → contribution to company → realistic ambition.

42. "Why should we hire you over other candidates?"
Framework: 3 specific differentiators, each tied to a job requirement.

43. "What's your expected salary?"
Framework: Research-based range, anchor high, give a range not a number.
```
"Based on my research and [X years] experience, I'm targeting 
$[X]–$[Y]. I'm open to the full comp conversation — 
is that range aligned with your budget?"
```

44. **"Do you have any questions for us?"**
→ See Part 4: 20 Questions to Ask the Interviewer.

45-50: **Company-specific questions** — See AI Prompt #3.

## PART 3: SALARY NEGOTIATION SCRIPTS

### Script 1: First offer received
```
"Thank you — I'm really excited about this opportunity. 
Before I respond, can I take [24-48 hours] to review the full offer?"

[After reviewing:]
"I've reviewed the offer. I'm very interested in joining. 
Based on my experience and market data, I was expecting something 
closer to $[X]. Is there flexibility there?"
```

### Script 2: Counter-offer
```
"I appreciate the offer of $[X]. Given [your unique value 1] and 
[unique value 2], I'd be comfortable at $[X+15-20%]. 
Can we meet there?"
```

### Script 3: They say the salary is fixed
```
"I understand. Are there other elements of compensation that have 
more flexibility — like [signing bonus / extra PTO / remote work / 
stock / professional development budget]?"
```

### Script 4: You have a competing offer
```
"I want to be transparent: I have another offer at $[X] that I need 
to respond to by [date]. You're my first choice — is there any way 
to get to $[Y]?"
```

### Script 5: Accepting the offer
```
"I'm excited to accept. I'd like to confirm: [salary], [start date], 
and [any negotiated terms]. Can you send an updated offer letter?"
```

## PART 4: 20 QUESTIONS TO ASK THE INTERVIEWER

**About the role:**
1. "What does success look like in this role in the first 90 days?"
2. "What are the biggest challenges someone in this role typically faces?"
3. "How did this position become available?"
4. "What does a typical week look like for someone in this role?"
5. "What would you want this person to accomplish in their first year?"

**About the team:**
6. "How would you describe the team culture?"
7. "How does this team handle disagreements or conflict?"
8. "What's the collaboration style — mostly independent or highly collaborative?"
9. "What do you enjoy most about working here?"
10. "How does the team celebrate wins?"

**About the company:**
11. "Where do you see the company in 3 years?"
12. "What's the biggest challenge the company is currently facing?"
13. "How has the company evolved in the last 2 years?"
14. "How does leadership communicate with the broader team?"
15. "What's the company's approach to professional development?"

**About next steps:**
16. "What are the next steps in the process?"
17. "What's your timeline for making a decision?"
18. "Is there anything from our conversation that gives you hesitation about my fit?"
19. "What does the onboarding process look like?"
20. "What separates candidates who succeed here from those who don't?"

## PART 5: AI PROMPTS FOR INTERVIEW PREP

### Prompt 1: Generate custom STAR stories
```
My background: [brief description of experience, industry, role].
The job I'm interviewing for: [job title + company type + key responsibilities].
Generate 5 specific behavioral interview questions likely to be asked,
and for each, create a STAR-P answer framework I can customize.
```

### Prompt 2: Practice mock interview
```
You are a senior [job title] interviewer at a [company type].
I'm interviewing for [role]. Interview me for 10 questions.
After each answer I give, score it 1-10 and give specific feedback:
what worked, what to improve, what detail to add.
Start with: "Tell me about yourself."
```

### Prompt 3: Company-specific question prep
```
I'm interviewing at [Company name]. Their product is [description]. 
Their recent news: [any news you know about]. 
Generate 10 company-specific questions they're likely to ask, 
and a framework for answering each.
```

### Prompt 4: Weakness answer
```
My actual weakness is [real weakness].
Help me craft a "weakness" interview answer that:
1. Is honest (not fake)
2. Shows self-awareness
3. Demonstrates I'm actively improving it
4. Doesn't raise a red flag for this specific role: [role description]
```

### Prompt 5: Salary research
```
I'm interviewing for [job title] at a [company size] company in 
[city/remote]. I have [X] years experience. 
What salary range should I target? 
What are the key factors that justify the high end of that range?
Give me a negotiation script for my specific situation.
```

### Prompt 6: Thank-you email after interview
```
I just interviewed for [role] at [company]. 
The interviewer's name is [name]. 
During the interview we discussed [2-3 specific topics].
Write a 100-word thank-you email that references those specifics, 
reaffirms my interest, and adds one brief value statement.
```

### Prompt 7: Rejection recovery
```
I was rejected for [role] at [company].
Write a professional response email that:
1. Thanks them graciously
2. Asks for one specific piece of feedback
3. Leaves the door open for future opportunities
Keep it under 80 words.
```

## PART 6: THE 7-DAY INTERVIEW PREP PLAN

**Day 1 — Research**
- [ ] Read company website, About page, recent news
- [ ] Research the interviewer(s) on LinkedIn
- [ ] Understand the product/service from a user perspective
- [ ] Identify company challenges and recent initiatives

**Day 2 — Your Story**
- [ ] Write your "Tell me about yourself" answer (90 seconds)
- [ ] Identify your top 5 career achievements with metrics
- [ ] Prepare your "greatest weakness" answer
- [ ] Prepare your "why this company" answer

**Day 3 — STAR Stories**
- [ ] Write 5 STAR-P stories covering: leadership, failure, conflict, achievement, learning
- [ ] Ensure each story has a specific metric in the Result step
- [ ] Practice out loud until natural (not memorized)

**Day 4 — Role-Specific Prep**
- [ ] Re-read the job description word by word
- [ ] Map each requirement to a story or example from your background
- [ ] Prepare answers for role-specific questions (Part 2)
- [ ] Research industry trends relevant to the role

**Day 5 — Mock Interview**
- [ ] Use AI Prompt #2 for a full mock interview
- [ ] Record yourself on video — watch it back
- [ ] Identify nervous habits (filler words, looking away, rushing)
- [ ] Practice until the content flows naturally

**Day 6 — Logistics**
- [ ] Confirm time, format (video/in-person), interviewer name(s)
- [ ] Prepare questions to ask (choose 5 from Part 4)
- [ ] Research salary range (AI Prompt #5)
- [ ] Prepare what to wear, test tech if remote

**Day 7 — Final Polish**
- [ ] Light review of key stories (don't over-prepare)
- [ ] Sleep 8 hours
- [ ] Morning: exercise, eat well, arrive/log in early
- [ ] Send thank-you email within 2 hours of interview

## QUICK REFERENCE CARD

**Any behavioral question:** STAR-P framework
**Any weakness question:** Real + self-aware + actively improving
**Any salary question:** Range, not a number. Anchor 15-20% high.

**After:** Thank-you email within 2 hours, reference specific conversation
