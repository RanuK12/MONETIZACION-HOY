## Part 1 — The ATS Killer Framework

ATS (Applicant Tracking Systems) reject 75% of resumes before a human sees them. Here's how to beat it.

### The 5 ATS Rules

**Rule 1: Mirror the job description exactly**
If the posting says "revenue growth", your resume says "revenue growth" — not "sales increase".

**Rule 2: No tables, columns, or text boxes**
ATS can't parse them. Use single-column layout only.

**Rule 3: Spell out acronyms once**
"Search Engine Optimization (SEO)" — not just "SEO".

**Rule 4: Use standard section headers**
- Work Experience (not "Career Journey")
- Education (not "Academic Background")
- Skills (not "Toolbox")

**Rule 5: .docx beats PDF for ATS**
Send PDF to humans, .docx to ATS systems.

## Part 2 — The STAR-R Bullet Formula

Every bullet point on your resume should follow this structure:

**S — Situation** (optional, 3 words max)
**T — Task** (what you were responsible for)
**A — Action** (what you specifically did)
**R — Result** (measurable outcome)
**R — Relevance** (to the role you're applying for)

### Before / After Examples

**Before (weak):**
> Helped with marketing campaigns

**After (STAR-R):**
> Led 3 email campaigns targeting SMB segment → 34% open rate, 2.1x industry average, drove $180K pipeline in Q3

---

**Before:**
> Managed social media

**After:**
> Grew LinkedIn following 0 → 12K in 8 months by publishing weekly data-driven threads → 3 inbound demo requests/week

---

**Before:**
> Worked on customer success

**After:**
> Reduced churn from 8.2% → 3.1% in 6 months by building proactive health score dashboard in Salesforce → saved $420K ARR

## Part 4 — The Summary Block Templates

The summary (top 3 lines) is the most important section. It's the only thing recruiters always read.

### Template A — The "T-shaped" Professional
```
[Title] with [X] years in [domain], specializing in [niche skill].
Built [notable thing] at [company/context] that [measurable result].
Now looking to [bring specific value] to a [type of company/role].
```

**Example:**
```
Growth marketer with 4 years in B2B SaaS, specializing in product-led acquisition.
Built the referral loop at Acme that drove 38% of signups with $0 ad spend.
Looking to bring the same scrappy, data-first approach to an early-stage Series A.
```

### Template B — The Career Switcher
```
Former [old role] transitioning to [new role] with [transferable skill].
[Specific bridge project or certification that proves seriousness].
Brings [unique perspective from previous career] that most [new field] candidates don't have.
```

### Template C — The New Grad
```
[Degree] graduate with hands-on experience in [2-3 relevant skills].
Built [project] during [context] — [specific result].
Seeking [role type] where I can [specific contribution].
```

## Part 5 — The Skills Section Formula

**Wrong:**
> Skills: Microsoft Office, Communication, Teamwork, Leadership, Excel

**Right (3-column, keyword-rich):**
```
Tools:        Salesforce | HubSpot | Notion | Figma | Looker
Languages:    Python | SQL | JavaScript
Methods:      Agile/Scrum | OKR frameworks | A/B testing | GTM strategy
```

Rule: Only list skills you can defend in an interview. One question about a skill you listed poorly beats your resume.

## Part 7 — The 7-Day Interview Checklist

**Day 1:** Rewrite summary block + run ATS audit
**Day 2:** Upgrade top 5 bullet points with STAR-R
**Day 3:** Tailor resume to 3 target companies (job descriptions)
**Day 4:** Rewrite LinkedIn headline + about section
**Day 5:** Apply to 10 roles with tailored resume
**Day 6:** Follow up on applications older than 5 days
**Day 7:** Review what's getting opens (if using tracking)

Repeat weekly until you have 3 interviews scheduled.
